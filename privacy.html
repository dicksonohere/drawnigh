In short

Nothing leaves your device unless you turn on sync. Then it goes only to your own Google Drive — never to us. We never see it.

What DrawNigh keeps

Your reading, stars, Gems, streak, plans, settings, and anything you write in The Secret Place are kept on your own device. DrawNigh has no server, no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Sync (off unless you turn it on)

When you turn on sync and sign in with Google, DrawNigh saves a copy of your reading into your own Google Drive, in a hidden folder.

DrawNigh asks Google for one permission only: to use its own hidden folder. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive. It does not ask for your emails, contacts or photos.

The Secret Place syncs only if you turn that on too. Its copy is sealed with your word before it leaves your device.

Your copy goes between your device and Google only. It never passes through us.

The New Living Translation

With the NLT switched on, DrawNigh asks Tyndale for the chapter you are reading, and nothing else about you.

Removing your data

When you turn sync off, DrawNigh offers to remove your Drive copy.

You can also remove it any time yourself: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → DrawNigh → Delete hidden app data.

To take away DrawNigh's permission: your Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps.

What is on your device goes when you remove DrawNigh or clear its data.

Google's rules

DrawNigh's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
