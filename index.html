Chime volume

Quiet enough for a desk with someone sitting across it. If you are still praying when it sounds, stay praying — it is a notice, not a summons.

A sleeping phone switches this app off, and a switched-off app cannot sound. So the screen is held awake while a prayer runs — only while it runs. Lock the phone yourself and you have overruled that: the chime waits, and lands when you pick it up.

While a prayer runs the screen stays on. In a pocket it can take stray taps.

Show translators' notes

The 1611 translators left notes in the margin — what the Hebrew or Greek says literally, or an alternative reading. They are kept out of the verse itself; switch this on to see them underneath.

When it has been a while

After a few days with nothing read, the browsing card comes round this often instead of the usual wait — one verse, in front of you, sooner. Read anything at all and it goes back to normal immediately.

Verse card on this screen

On a computer the browsing verse card can be dragged wherever you like, and stays there. On a phone it always sits at the bottom.

Backup & Restore

Your progress lives only on this device. Copy a backup code before switching browsers or phones. Codes from the original Mini BitBible app work here too.

A saved file survives a phone you no longer have. It goes wherever your browser puts downloads, and your phone may back it up to Google or iCloud — encrypted and useless to them, but there. That is why it survives.

Merge adds the code's reading to this device — nothing is ever lost, so it is safe in both directions and an old code can do no harm. Replace wipes this device and installs the code instead; use it on a new phone.
