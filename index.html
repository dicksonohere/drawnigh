Backup & Restore

Your progress lives only on this device. Copy a backup code before switching browsers or phones. Codes from the original Mini BitBible app work here too.

A saved file survives a phone you no longer have. It goes wherever your browser puts downloads, and your phone may back it up to Google or iCloud — encrypted and useless to them, but there. That is why it survives.

Merge adds the code's reading to this device — nothing is ever lost, so it is safe in both directions and an old code can do no harm. Replace wipes this device and installs the code instead; use it on a new phone.

🔊 Read aloud

Your phone's own voice reads the verse. No voice is downloaded and nothing about you leaves the device. A verse heard is a verse read — it counts toward your day, your streak and your plan, exactly like reading with your eyes.

Stop at the end of each chapter

The chapter stop is here so a phone reading to an empty room stops on its own. Turn it off and the reading runs to the end of the book — and everything it reads is counted, heard or not.

Hold the screen awake while it reads

To keep the light on, press and hold the listen button. An ordinary press reads aloud and lets the phone sleep — the voice carries on in the dark, which is what you want while cooking or driving. Hold the button instead and the screen stays lit for the whole sitting, through every chapter, until you stop the reading. Some browsers will not hold the screen; where that happens the app says so.

🚪 The Secret Place

A room inside DrawNigh, behind a verse you choose, a knock you choose and a word you choose. The three locks guard this screen; the device itself is what guards the writing. Anyone who can already read this phone's files can read what is in the room — what travels in your backup code is sealed, and that is the copy that gets pasted, photographed and emailed. “But thou, when thou prayest, enter into thy closet, and when thou hast shut thy door, pray to thy Father which is in secret.” — Matthew 6:6

Your word

Make a room

Choose the verse that is your door. Any verse in the KJV — one of 31,100, and nothing will mark it.

Now the knock, and it is a number. 4567 means: let the prayer clock run 4 seconds and pause it · run 5 and pause · run 6 and pause · run 7 and pause. Three to six digits, each between 1 and 9.

Counting starts
Nobody but you knows which of the two you chose. Starting after your first pause also lets you reach the room in the middle of a prayer you are already praying.
Let me be out by

When the last pause is done, resume the prayer and simply pray. After five unbroken seconds the word panel appears. Do not tap again — a man tapping his way in never stops, and that is exactly what keeps him out.

Tap to practise — the seconds run here

The word is the one nobody can ever recover. Not the app, not anyone. No reset link, no recovery code, no back door — the same thing that means no server and no surveillance means no rescue. Forget the word and the room is closed for good.

This room lives in your browser's own storage. Clearing your browsing data removes it, and a phone short on space can remove it too. Your backup code is the only copy that survives that.

Your verse and your knock are a different matter. Both can be changed from inside the room, and a backup code gets you past either one — they travel inside its seal, so a device restoring your code asks for the word alone. That is what a backup code is for. Make one, and keep it.
